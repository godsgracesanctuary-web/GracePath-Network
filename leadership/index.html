Leadership & Governance

GracePath Network is a Christ-centered ministry dedicated to equipping believers, churches, ministries, families, leaders, and communities through biblical teaching, discipleship resources, leadership development, mentorship, and practical tools that inspire spiritual growth and kingdom impact.

Who We Are

GracePath Network is a Christ-centered learning, discipleship, leadership, and resource platform committed to helping individuals grow spiritually, discover purpose, and impact their generation for Christ.

We provide churches, ministries, Christian educators, families, and believers with discipleship materials, Bible study guides, leadership courses, devotional resources, children's lessons, youth programs, family enrichment materials, and practical ministry tools that strengthen faith and encourage spiritual maturity.

GracePath Network is also passionate about helping young people identify, develop, and maximize their God-given gifts, talents, abilities, and potential. Through mentorship, training, encouragement, and practical opportunities, we seek to inspire young men and women to pursue excellence, innovation, service, and purposeful living.

By utilizing digital media, collaborative ministry, and biblical teaching, we desire to strengthen local churches, empower ministries, equip leaders, encourage families, and raise a generation of Christ-centered individuals who positively influence their communities and the world.

Founder & Executive Director

Founder & Executive Director

David Ngure founded GracePath Network with a vision of creating accessible biblical resources that support discipleship, leadership growth, family transformation, and ministry effectiveness.

His passion is to see believers mature in faith, leaders equipped for service, churches strengthened, ministries empowered, and communities positively influenced through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
